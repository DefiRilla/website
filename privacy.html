1. Who We Are & Data Controller Identity

This Privacy Policy applies to the website, platform, and alerting services accessible via DeFiRilla.com (collectively, the "Platform"), operated by Brightstart Nominees PTY LTD trading as DeFiRilla (referred to as "DeFiRilla," "we," "our," or "us"), registered in Australia at 102 Victoria Street, Carlton, Melbourne, Victoria 3053, Australia.

For the purposes of applicable data protection laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), Brightstart Nominees PTY LTD is responsible for the processing of personal information described in this Policy and acts as a data controller where applicable under relevant privacy laws. If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, contact our Privacy Contact at support [at] DeFiRilla.com.

The GDPR and UK GDPR apply to DeFiRilla only to the extent required by their respective territorial scopes, including where DeFiRilla offers goods or services to individuals in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, or monitors the behaviour of individuals located there. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, DeFiRilla will comply with the requirements applicable to the relevant processing. References to GDPR rights and obligations in this Policy apply only where and to the extent the GDPR or UK GDPR is legally applicable to the relevant processing.

2. Platform Overview & Read-Only Architecture

DeFiRilla provides a passive, read-only monitoring and alerting tool for decentralised finance (DeFi) positions across EVM-compatible blockchain networks. DeFiRilla enables users to receive automated risk notifications (including Health Factor changes, LP active range exits, and yield fluctuations) without requiring wallet connections or transaction signatures.

Read-Only Security Commitment

  • No Wallet Connections: DeFiRilla will never ask you to connect your Web3 wallet (e.g., MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet).
  • No Signatures or Approvals: DeFiRilla never requests transaction signatures, message signing, or smart contract token approvals.
  • Zero Access to Assets or Private Keys: DeFiRilla cannot access, hold, transfer, trade, stake, or manage private keys, seed phrases, or user funds under any circumstances.
  • Alerting Tripwires: Alerting Tripwires are wallet-monitoring rules that trigger automated risk notifications when specified on-chain conditions change significantly, including changes to Health Factor, LP active-range status, and supply, borrow, or reward APRs.

3. Information We Process

Depending on how you interact with the Platform, we process the following categories of information:

A. Information Provided by You

  • Public Wallet Addresses: Public EVM wallet addresses that you submit for on-chain position monitoring.
  • Notification Destinations: Your Telegram Chat ID or username provided through our bot setup, or your email address, used to route alerts to your chosen destination.

B. Information Automatically Processed (Usage & Technical Data)

  • Technical & Server Logs: Standard server and application error logs, including IP addresses, access timestamps, user-agent details (browser type and OS), and request headers, processed on infrastructure managed by DeFiRilla and provided by third-party cloud infrastructure providers for security, system stability, and abuse prevention.
  • Pseudonymous Analytics: If you opt in to non-essential analytics cookies, we collect pseudonymous usage and traffic metrics through Google Analytics to evaluate traffic, usage patterns, and system performance.

C. Public Blockchain Information

Publicly available, on-chain data associated with submitted wallet addresses, including token balances, borrowing/lending Health Factors, liquidity pool positions, and smart contract interactions.

Public blockchain data is obtained from publicly accessible blockchain infrastructure services used by DeFiRilla to query and monitor those networks.

Where we process publicly available blockchain information relating to non-users, we do so for security, service operation, and improvement under legitimate interests, subject to a documented balancing assessment. Where required by applicable law, we provide Article 14 information through this Policy and, where appropriate and feasible, direct notice. Where Article 14(5)(b) applies because individual notice would be impossible or involve disproportionate effort, DeFiRilla will document the assessment, implement appropriate measures to protect data subjects' rights and interests, and make the relevant information publicly available through this Policy.

D. Raw Blockchain Data vs. DeFiRilla Derived Data

  • Raw On-Chain Data: Public ledger state that exists independently on decentralised networks.
  • DeFiRilla Derived Data: Calculations, risk state evaluations, Health Factor alerts, LP active range statuses, and APR tracking generated by our internal monitoring engine.

E. Sources and Indirect Collection

We collect information directly from you when you submit a wallet address, configure alerts, provide a notification destination or contact us. We also collect information indirectly from public blockchain ledgers and from third-party blockchain infrastructure, RPC and indexing providers used to retrieve on-chain data. These providers may receive wallet addresses, network and query information necessary to return blockchain data. We may also receive information about blockchain addresses or transactions relating to individuals who have not directly interacted with DeFiRilla. This may include publicly available blockchain addresses, transaction records, token or protocol activity, and related identifiers. We process this information to operate, secure, analyse, and improve our read-only monitoring and alerting services, retain it only as described in Section 10, and respond to applicable rights requests subject to legal limitations.

4. Wallet Re-Identification & Public Blockchain Disclaimer

Pseudonymity Notice: Public wallet addresses are pseudonymous identifiers. While DeFiRilla does not require your legal name or identity, wallet addresses may become re-identifiable when combined with third-party data sources, public social profiles, or notification endpoints.

Blockchain Immutability: Public blockchain networks are decentralised, transparent, and permanent public ledgers. DeFiRilla does not control, alter, or erase public blockchain data.

10. Data Retention Policy

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the operational purposes for which it was collected:

  • Wallet Addresses & Notification Destinations: Wallet addresses and notification destinations are retained while the associated alerting tripwire remains active, subject to a maximum initial retention period of 12 months from creation. At the end of that period, the tripwire automatically expires and its associated data is scheduled for deletion unless the user expressly confirms renewal through the Platform before expiry. Reactivation of a cancelled or expired tripwire is permitted only through the Platform and establishes a new 12-month retention period from the date of reactivation, subject to applicable deletion processes. Each qualifying renewal or reactivation establishes a new 12-month retention period from the date of confirmation or reactivation. Receiving or acknowledging an alert does not constitute renewal. Qualifying renewal occurs only when the user expressly confirms renewal through the Platform, or reactivates a cancelled or expired tripwire through the Platform. The 12-month renewal period begins on the date of that confirmation or reactivation, which DeFiRilla records in the alert configuration history. Following a deletion request or alert cancellation, we delete the relevant wallet address, notification destination, alert configuration, alert settings, and notification-destination linkage from active production systems and application databases within 30 days; copies in DeFiRilla-controlled encrypted backups and disaster-recovery systems are deleted or overwritten within 90 days after the deletion request or alert cancellation, unless a longer retention period is required by law. A documented active investigation may extend these periods only for the minimum additional period necessary, with the reason, data affected, owner, and review date recorded; the extension will end when the investigation concludes or the legal obligation expires, whichever occurs first. Information held by independent third-party providers is subject to their retention practices; where DeFiRilla can request deletion, it will do so promptly, but DeFiRilla cannot guarantee deletion within 90 days. Security logs containing personal information are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for security monitoring, incident investigation, legal compliance, or dispute resolution, and ordinarily for no longer than 12 months unless a documented longer period is legally required or necessary for an active investigation. Independent third-party providers may apply their own documented retention periods. The current provider list, provider roles, data processed, and processing purposes are described in Section 8. Applicable retention periods are specified in this Section 10 or, where determined by a provider's independent retention policy, identified in the provider-specific information available on request. Where DeFiRilla controls the data or can request its deletion, we will promptly submit deletion requests following cancellation or a valid deletion request. If alerts remain undeliverable for one continuous month after reasonable delivery-retry procedures, DeFiRilla may deactivate the tripwire and delete its associated data, notwithstanding the ordinary 12-month maximum retention period. Mere non-renewal or failure to acknowledge an otherwise successfully delivered alert does not trigger early deletion.
  • Blockchain Query Logs & Technical Server Logs: Ordinary blockchain-query and technical server logs used for system stability, debugging, and routine auditing are retained for a maximum of 90 days and then automatically deleted. Only logs formally designated as security logs under DeFiRilla's documented log-classification policy qualify for the extended security-log retention period. The designation must record the security purpose, data categories, responsible owner, creation date, and scheduled deletion date. Ordinary blockchain-query, debugging, and routine-audit logs must not be reclassified as security logs solely to extend retention. Security logs are retained only for the minimum period necessary for security monitoring, incident investigation, legal compliance, or dispute resolution, ordinarily no longer than 12 months, and are automatically deleted when the applicable retention period expires.
  • Analytics Data: Retained for 14 months. At the end of this period, Google Analytics data is automatically deleted in accordance with the configured retention settings. Where Google Analytics provides anonymisation rather than deletion for a particular data element, that data is anonymised in accordance with the applicable configuration and Google Analytics processing practices.
  • Indirectly Collected Public Blockchain Data: Public blockchain information relating to non-users and other indirectly collected blockchain data is reviewed at least every 12 months and deleted or anonymised when no longer necessary for the documented security, service operation, analysis, or improvement purpose for which it is retained. Unless retention is required for an active security investigation, legal compliance, or dispute resolution, such data is retained for no longer than 24 months. Where specific retention periods apply to associated technical logs or derived data, those periods are described in this Section.
