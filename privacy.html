2. Platform Overview & Read-Only Architecture

DeFiRilla provides a passive, read-only monitoring and alerting tool for decentralised finance (DeFi) positions across blockchain networks compatible with the Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM). DeFiRilla enables users to receive automated risk notifications (including changes in a position's Health Factor, liquidity-provider (LP) active range exits, and yield fluctuations) without requiring wallet connections or transaction signatures.

Read-Only Security Commitment

  • No Wallet Connections: DeFiRilla will never ask you to connect your Web3 wallet (e.g., MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet).
  • No Signatures or Approvals: DeFiRilla never requests transaction signatures, message signing, or smart contract token approvals.
  • No Access to Assets or Private Keys: The DeFiRilla Platform is designed and operated without functionality that permits DeFiRilla to access, hold, transfer, trade, stake, or manage users' private keys, seed phrases, or funds.

An 'Alerting Tripwire' (also referred to as an 'alert' or 'alert configuration') is a wallet-monitoring rule that triggers automated risk notifications when specified on-chain conditions change significantly, including changes to Health Factor, LP active-range status, and supply, borrow, or reward APRs.

C. Public Blockchain Information

Publicly available, on-chain data associated with submitted wallet addresses, including token balances, borrowing/lending Health Factor values, liquidity pool positions, and smart contract interactions.

Public blockchain data is obtained from publicly accessible blockchain infrastructure services used by DeFiRilla to query and monitor those networks.

Where we process publicly available blockchain information relating to non-users, we do so for security, service operation, and improvement under legitimate interests, subject to a documented balancing assessment. Where required by applicable law, we provide Article 14 information through this Policy and, where appropriate and feasible, direct notice. D. Raw Blockchain Data vs. DeFiRilla Derived Data

  • Raw On-Chain Data: Public ledger state that exists independently on decentralised networks.
  • DeFiRilla Derived Data: Calculations, risk state evaluations, Health Factor alerts, liquidity-provider (LP) active range statuses, and APR tracking generated by our internal monitoring engine.

10. Data Retention Policy

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the operational purposes for which it was collected:

  • Wallet Addresses & Notification Destinations: (a) Retention period: Wallet addresses and notification destinations are retained while the associated Alerting Tripwire remains active, subject to a maximum initial retention period of 12 months from creation. (b) Expiry and deletion: At the end of that period, the tripwire automatically expires and, except for the dispute-resolution records described below, its associated data is deleted from active production systems and application databases within 30 days after expiry, and from DeFiRilla-controlled backups and disaster-recovery systems within 90 days after expiry, unless the user expressly confirms renewal through the Platform before expiry. (c) Renewal and reactivation: Each qualifying renewal establishes a new 12-month retention period from the date of confirmation; reactivation of an expired or cancelled tripwire establishes a new 12-month retention period only from the date of reactivation, provided the configuration has not yet been deleted from active production systems (deletion from backups does not extend the reactivation period). Receiving or acknowledging an alert does not constitute renewal. (d) Deletion on request or cancellation: Following a deletion request or alert cancellation, and except for dispute-resolution records, we delete the relevant wallet address, notification destination, alert configuration, alert settings, and notification-destination linkage from active production systems within 30 days, and from encrypted backups within 90 days, unless a specific law requires longer retention. (e) Investigation extension: A documented active investigation may extend an applicable retention period by no more than 12 months beyond the stated period; no legal-compliance or dispute-resolution exception permits retention beyond that combined maximum unless a specific law, regulation or court order expressly requires longer retention, in which case the data is retained only for the period that law, regulation or order requires. (f) Third-party providers: Information held by third-party providers identified as independent controllers is subject to their retention practices; where DeFiRilla can request deletion, it will do so promptly but cannot guarantee deletion within 90 days. (g) Undeliverable alerts: If alerts remain undeliverable for one continuous month after reasonable delivery-retry procedures, DeFiRilla may deactivate the Alerting Tripwire and will delete its associated data within 30 days of deactivation; this deadline does not extend any other applicable retention period. Mere non-renewal or failure to acknowledge an otherwise successfully delivered alert does not trigger early deletion.
  • Blockchain Query Logs & Technical Server Logs: Ordinary blockchain-query and technical server logs used for system stability, debugging, and routine auditing are retained for a maximum of 90 days and then automatically deleted. Only logs formally designated as security logs under DeFiRilla's documented log-classification policy qualify for retention beyond 90 days; all other blockchain-query and technical server logs must be deleted within 90 days. The designation must record the security purpose, data categories, responsible owner, creation date, and scheduled deletion date. Ordinary blockchain-query, debugging, and routine-audit logs must not be reclassified as security logs solely to extend retention. Security logs are retained only for the minimum period necessary for security monitoring, incident investigation, legal compliance, or dispute resolution, and in no event longer than 12 months from creation; the only permitted extensions are a documented active investigation extension of no more than 12 months (for a combined maximum of 24 months) or retention expressly required by a specific law or court order; security logs are automatically deleted when the applicable retention period expires.
  • Analytics Data: DeFiRilla retains analytics data for 14 months. At the end of this period, data subject to deletion under the configured Google Analytics retention settings is deleted; where Google Analytics provides anonymisation rather than deletion for a particular data element, that element is anonymised instead, in accordance with the applicable configuration and Google Analytics processing practices.
  • Dispute Resolution & Record-Keeping Data: Following alert cancellation, expiry or a deletion request, the wallet address, notification destination and alert configuration history needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims are retained in a restricted-access dispute-resolution archive for a maximum of 12 months, and are then permanently deleted or irreversibly anonymised, unless extended under the investigation rule stated in the Wallet Addresses & Notification Destinations bullet of this Section.
  • Indirectly Collected Public Blockchain Data: Public blockchain information relating to non-users and other indirectly collected blockchain data is retained only in the minimum fields and at the granularity necessary for a documented purpose of security, service operation, or service improvement. DeFiRilla records the purpose, data fields, start date, responsible owner, review date, and deletion or anonymisation trigger for each retention category; reviews each category at least every 12 months and deletes or anonymises the data when that purpose ends; in all cases, no category may be retained for more than 24 months, except that a documented active investigation may extend the applicable period only in accordance with the single 12-month investigation-extension cap stated above. Where specific retention periods apply to associated technical logs or derived data, those periods are described in this Section.
